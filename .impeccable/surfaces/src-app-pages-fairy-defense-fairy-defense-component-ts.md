## Product boundary

This playtest contains only 9 + 1 = 10, then 8 + 2 = 10. Thinking time is unlimited; mistakes advance the same monster, every third mistake costs a heart, and losing three hearts offers retry. Success transforms the monster into a butterfly. Wave 1 celebration offers replay or return; it awards no stars, mastery, level completion or chapter rewards. Decorative star-wand effects are not earned stars.

Pause contains music, effects, speech and language controls. Playtest preferences and tutorial state use separate storage from learning progress. See `docs/fairy-wave-one-playtest.md` for play instructions, rules, verification and artwork provenance.
