# Feenland Mathezauber

A touch-first math adventure for children, with responsive phone, tablet and desktop layouts. The user-approved reference is a fairyland with a red-haired fairy, green leaf dress, cream surfaces, dark blue text and pink primary actions. Keep artwork simple in the first delivery.

The Home screen offers Continue/Start, Explore Worlds, cumulative achievements and a Settings gear. Launch languages are en/de/es/fr; language selection lives in Settings. Content follows World → Chapter → Level → Challenge with stable IDs. Progress stays locally and earned accomplishments survive catalog growth. All old unicorn, berry and number-bridge content is removed by explicit user request.

Current delivery: Home, World Map, World Detail chapter paths, Chapter Detail level selection, challenge previews, result-driven completion, and language settings. Placeholder completions are clearly labeled demos and saved separately from real learning mastery and chapter awards. Fairy Tower Defense is a complete twenty-wave making-ten adventure with sequential unlocks, completed-wave replay, shield bosses at waves 5/10/15/20, guided early practice and optional counting help. Spells expand to 0–10 from wave 11, and independent prompts shorten from wave 13. Wave-boundary saves support multiple sittings; only finishing all twenty waves enters the existing real completion flow for stars and mastery. Thinking time is unlimited. No fake learning achievements, timer, accounts, analytics or cloud sync.
