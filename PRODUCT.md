# Feenland Mathezauber

A touch-first math adventure for children, with responsive phone, tablet and desktop layouts. The user-approved reference is a fairyland with a red-haired fairy, green leaf dress, cream surfaces, dark blue text and pink primary actions. Keep artwork simple in the first delivery.

The Home screen offers Continue/Start, Explore Worlds, cumulative achievements and a Settings gear. Launch languages are en/de/es/fr; language selection lives in Settings. Content follows World → Chapter → Level → Challenge with stable IDs. Progress stays locally and earned accomplishments survive catalog growth. All old unicorn, berry and number-bridge content is removed by explicit user request.

Current delivery includes the twenty-wave Fairy Tower Defense campaign, plus nine selectable math stages covering tens and ones, addition, subtraction and mixed practice up to 30. Each stage displays its skill, range and example. Difficulty and pace are independent remembered preferences. Real practice completion is saved separately from demos. Existing campaign saves remain valid.

The Numbers world also offers The Hungry Crocodile, a six-level comparison game built around the classroom trick that the crocodile's open mouth always eats the bigger number. Levels move from comparing numbers up to 20 and 100, to adding or subtracting before comparing, to two calculations on either side, and finally mixed numbers and sums up to 100. Each round feeds the crocodile ten snacks; a wrong pick shows comparison bars and both results as help, two or more stars open the next level, and finishing always counts.
